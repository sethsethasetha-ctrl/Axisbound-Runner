Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Axisbound Runner is provided by CWY. This policy explains how the app handles information when you play the game.

Local Game Data

The app stores game progress, unlocked levels, scores, settings, and audio preferences locally on your device. This information is used to provide game features and is not intended to identify you directly.

Advertising and Tracking Permission

The app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before using an advertising identifier. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use device identifiers and related information to deliver or measure ads. If you decline, the game remains playable and the app continues without using the advertising identifier for that purpose.

Advertising and Security Services

The app can use third-party advertising services, including Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi, to request and present ads. These services may process device, network, and advertising-related information under their own privacy policies. The app also uses a security service to help protect the app and its services; it may process a device vendor identifier and technical device or network information for security purposes.

Audio

Background music and sound-effect settings are stored locally. The app does not upload your music preferences.

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party advertising service, please contact that service directly and contact us so we can assist.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
